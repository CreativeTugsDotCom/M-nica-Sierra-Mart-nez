About Mónica

“Understanding the person within the story.”

As an Oaxacan psychologist and psychotherapist, my practice is rooted in the belief that our emotional lives are deeply shaped not only by individual experiences, but also by the intricate web of relationships, family dynamics, personal history, and the wider systems around us.

Therapy is a collaborative sanctuary—a space to slow down, examine recurring patterns, and gently untangle what feels heavy. Whether you are navigating a personal life transition, relationship strain, or deep-seated family dynamics, my commitment is to offer a thoughtful, rigorous, and compassionate container for your healing process.

Grounded in professional expertise and profound respect for human dignity, I work alongside individuals, couples, and families to foster reconciliation, clarity, and sustainable personal transformation.

Therapeutic Framework

“A systemic and transgenerational perspective.”

Human emotional experience cannot be fully understood in isolation. Our work explores the relational frameworks and generational histories that inform how we love, struggle, and grow.

01

Systemic Perspective

Understanding people within the relationships, environments, and contexts that shape their daily lives and emotional realities.

02

Transgenerational Perspective

Exploring how family histories, inherited patterns, and ancestral experiences may influence the ways we relate and respond today.

03

Emotionally Focused Therapy

Creating safe therapeutic space to examine core emotional experiences and reshape the patterns that govern vital relationships.

04

Trauma-Informed Systemic Work

Approaching trauma with delicate attention to both individual nervous system responses and surrounding relational structures.
